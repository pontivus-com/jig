## Rules

1. Responsive differences are **presentation ownership**, never duplicate Task/Planner/synchronization/domain state.
2. Exactly one desktop or narrow configured presentation tree is mounted.
3. A narrow fixed/floating surface may not render in desktop mode.
4. A desktop hierarchy may not be compressed into narrow mode when its placement, primitive or touch geometry violates this matrix.
5. One semantic action may have two viewport-specific presentations only when this matrix declares that split.
6. Shared leaves may adapt width, wrapping, spacing, density and hit geometry; they may not synthesize an alternate semantic hierarchy with CSS.
7. Astryx adaptive primitives remain preferred when one primitive genuinely preserves the same semantics/focus/placement contract. Jig splits the presentation when the primitive or surrounding composition materially differs.
8. Every new v1.1.2 surface must be classified here before implementation.
9. Every row receives desktop + narrow real-browser acceptance before v1.1.2 release.
